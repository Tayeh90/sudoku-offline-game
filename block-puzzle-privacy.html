Block Puzzle: City Trip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

ELECTRAGLOBE PTY LTD ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Block Puzzle: City Trip (package name com.gridly.blockpuzzle) on Google Play and the Apple App Store. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have when you play the game.

By using the game, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

2. Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account or give us your name, email address or phone number to play. Your game progress (levels reached, coins, settings) is stored on your device.

Limited technical and usage information is collected automatically by the game and by the third-party services listed below. Depending on your device, settings and consent choices, this may include:

  • Device information such as device model, operating system version, language, country and general device settings
  • Gameplay and usage events such as levels started and completed, game mode chosen, session length and screens viewed
  • Crash reports, diagnostics and performance data
  • An app instance ID and, where permitted, the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address by our advertising and analytics partners
  • Purchase confirmations for in-app purchases (we never receive your card or banking details)

3. How We Use Information

  • Run, maintain and improve the game, its levels and difficulty
  • Show advertisements that keep the game free
  • Deliver in-app purchases such as coin packs and "No Ads"
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs
  • Understand which features are used so we can make the game better

4. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following services, which process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads)
  • Google User Messaging Platform — collects your advertising consent where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and app stability
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase (through Unity IAP) — in-app purchases

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps · Firebase privacy

5. Advertising and Consent

Block Puzzle: City Trip is free and shows ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy in the game. On iOS, the game asks permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking.

You can also limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying No Ads removes banner and interstitial ads.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases (coin packs and No Ads) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation of the purchase so we can deliver it, and you can restore non-consumable purchases from Settings → Restore. We do not receive your full payment details.

7. Children's Privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at Info@lwf.my and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google according to Firebase's retention settings (analytics data is retained for up to 14 months). To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance, email Info@lwf.my with the subject "Block Puzzle data deletion".

9. Your Choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Ad Privacy
  • Limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in device settings
  • Turn off sound, music and vibration in Settings
  • Uninstall the game at any time

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do.
