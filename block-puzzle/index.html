How to play

Easy to learn, fun to master.

  1. Drag the blocks onto the board.
  2. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  3. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing turn after turn, for combos.
  4. The game ends when no block fits, so plan ahead!
